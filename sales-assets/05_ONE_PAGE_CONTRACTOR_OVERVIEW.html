How it works

  • Define the market. Project types, service areas, the qualification standard, calendar availability, capacity, and pricing — agreed first.
  • Generate demand. We create and manage the homeowner acquisition campaigns.
  • Qualify and book. We speak to the homeowner, check them against your standard, and book a specific date and time.
  • Attend and close. You turn up, run the estimate, and control the sale.

What counts as a qualified booked appointment

  • A real homeowner, property owner, or authorized decision-maker
  • Valid and reachable contact information
  • Property inside your approved service area
  • Requested one of your agreed services
  • Demonstrated genuine project interest
  • Agreed to a specific appointment date and time
  • Not generated through fraud, bots, purchased lists, or falsification
  • Complies with the duplicate, cancellation, rescheduling, no-show and exclusivity rules in your agreement

Your responsibilities

  • Attend the booked appointment
  • Provide the estimate
  • Run your own sales process and close the job
  • Flag any appointment that misses the standard inside the dispute window
  • Keep your calendar accurate and capacity available
  • Maintain licensing and insurance

What we do not promise

  • That every scheduled homeowner answers every follow-up call
  • That every homeowner keeps the appointment, or does not reschedule
  • That every appointment results in an estimate
  • That every estimate becomes a signed job
  • Any particular close rate, contract value, revenue, profit, or return on ad spend
  • That every appointment is exclusive — exclusivity depends on your agreement
