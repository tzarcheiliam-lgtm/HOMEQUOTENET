# Text Message Templates

**Sender identity:** Liam, HomeQuote Network

## Rules for SMS

- Keep every message under about 320 characters. Most should be far shorter.
- One idea, one ask. Never two questions in a text.
- Identify yourself in the first message of any new thread.
- Write like a person: no marketing voice, no emoji, no all-caps.
- Include opt-out language on the first message to a new contact.
- Never put a price in a text before it has been agreed on a call.

> **`[Needs review]` — compliance.** Business-to-business texting still carries TCPA and carrier (10DLC) obligations in the US. Before sending at volume: register your brand and campaign for A2P 10DLC, keep records of consent, honour STOP immediately, and have counsel review your consent language and calling windows.
